## 5. Printable practice papers (IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, not deployed)

**Why.** Demand: "navodaya mental ability question paper" 22,200 searches a month and "… questions pdf" 9,900 (Ubersuggest,
27 Nov). Scribd PDFs hold #1 and #6 for it. Free-coaching groups for rural children, whom the JNVST is designed for, often
share one phone for a class: a paper they can print is the resource they would use and link to. It also gives every
outreach message something concrete to offer.

**What.** `/exams/practice/{set}/paper` for every published practice set:
- every question with its figures, options lettered (A)–(D), questions grouped by section, each passage printed once;
- the set's practice marking (never presented as the exam's official scheme), name and date lines, instructions;
- an answer sheet on its own page, and the online test's address to check answers and read the reasons;
- English or Hindi (`?lang=hi` makes a shareable Hindi link; questions without a Hindi version print in English);
- **no answers printed**: they stay on the server until a test is scored, so leaderboard scores stay fair;
- "Print this paper or save it as a PDF" on each set's start screen; a GA4 event `paper_print` when it is used.

**Search engines.** The paper's canonical address is the set's own page, as Google advises for print versions: a link to
a paper counts for the test page. Not in the sitemap; no Quiz data on the paper.
