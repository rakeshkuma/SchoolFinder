Admission season moves fast: forms open, close and selection lists come out within weeks. Having your documents ready before the form opens is the easiest way to avoid last-minute stress.

This checklist covers what schools in India **commonly** ask for. Every school's admission notice lists its **exact** requirements and the formats it accepts, so treat this as a starting point.

> Requirements differ by state, school and class, and change from year to year. The school's official admission notice always takes precedence over this list.

## Documents for nursery and Class 1

| Document | Why schools ask for it |
| --- | --- |
| Birth certificate | Proof of the child's age, to check the age limit for the class |
| Proof of residence | Many schools give priority by distance; also used for state rules |
| Passport-size photographs of the child | For the form and school records |
| Photographs of parents or guardian | Some schools ask for these with the form |
| Identity document of parents | To confirm who is applying |
| Identity document of the child, where asked | Some states and schools require one |

**Age rules:** each state and UT notifies its own minimum age and cut-off date for pre-primary and Class 1. In line with the National Education Policy 2020, the Ministry of Education has asked states to set the **minimum age for Class 1 at six years**. Check your state's current notification before applying.

## Documents for higher classes

When a child moves from another school, schools usually also ask for:

- **Transfer certificate (TC)** from the previous school.
- **Last report card or mark sheet.**
- For a move from another state or board, a **counter-signed TC** may be needed. Ask the new school in advance.
- For **Class 11**, the Class 10 mark sheet; streams often depend on Class 10 results.

## Extra documents for reserved RTE seats

Under **Section 12(1)(c) of the Right to Education Act**, private unaided (non-minority) schools reserve **at least 25% of entry-level seats** for children from economically weaker sections (EWS) and disadvantaged groups. For these seats, you typically need:

- **Income certificate** (for EWS), issued by the competent authority in your state.
- **Caste or category certificate** (for disadvantaged groups).
- Any other certificate your state lists, such as a disability certificate.

The **state or UT** runs this process, usually through a central online application and draw, not the school itself. Watch your state education department's website for the dates.

## Other documents that may be needed

- **Medical or disability certificate**, where relevant to the child's needs or to a reserved seat.
- **Sibling or alumni proof**, if the school gives points for these.
- **Vaccination record**, which some schools ask to see.

## A simple way to stay ready

1. Make **three photocopies** of each document and keep a **scanned set** on your phone; many forms are online.
2. Check that the **child's name and date of birth match** across all documents. Mismatches are a common reason for delays.
3. Note each school's **last date** and fee in one list.
4. Keep the **originals** safe and carry them for verification when asked.
