Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) are residential schools run by Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti, an autonomous organisation under the Ministry of Education. Most districts in India have one. Children join in **Class 6**, through a single test: the **Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST)**.

This guide follows the **JNVST-2027 prospectus**, which covers admission to Class 6 for the 2027-28 session. Rules can change between years, so read the prospectus for your year before applying.

> Dates, age limits and rules in this article are from the JNVST-2027 prospectus, checked on 11 November 2026. Always confirm them in the current prospectus on the official NVS admission portal.

## Who can apply

A child can apply for JNVST-2027 only if **all** of these are true:

- The child is a **bona fide resident of the district** where the JNV is, and is studying **Class 5 in the 2026-27 session** in a government, government-aided or recognised school in that district (or has an NIOS "B" certificate, as the prospectus allows).
- The child was **born between 1 May 2015 and 31 July 2017** (both dates included).
- The child has studied **Classes 3, 4 and 5 for full academic sessions**. A child who skipped a class, or has already passed Class 5, is not eligible.
- The child has **not appeared in JNVST before**. Each candidate gets one attempt.

If you want a **rural-quota** seat, the child must have studied Classes 3, 4 and 5 in schools located in rural areas. Check how the prospectus defines a rural school before you fill in the form.

## How seats are shared

Seats are filled **district by district**, on merit in the test. The prospectus sets these reservations:

| Group | Share of seats |
| --- | --- |
| Rural candidates | At least 75% |
| Girls | At least one-third |
| Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes | In proportion to the district's population (at least 15% SC and 7.5% ST) |
| Other Backward Classes (Central list) | 27% |
| Divyang candidates | As per Government of India norms |

Selection is **provisional** until the child's documents are checked at the JNV.

## What the test looks like

JNVST is a single paper of **80 objective questions for 100 marks**, in **two hours**:

| Section | Questions | Marks | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mental Ability and Environmental Studies | 40 | 50 | 60 minutes |
| Arithmetic | 20 | 25 | 30 minutes |
| Language (reading comprehension) | 20 | 25 | 30 minutes |

- Each correct answer carries **1.25 marks**. There is **no negative marking**.
- A child must reach the **minimum qualifying marks in every section**: 14 in Mental Ability and EVS, 7 in Arithmetic and 7 in Language.
- Answers are marked on an OMR sheet with a blue or black ball-point pen. Divyang candidates get 40 extra minutes.
- The test is offered in English, Hindi and the regional languages listed in the prospectus.

## Fees

Registration for JNVST is **free**. In a JNV, Classes 6 to 8 are free. From Class 9 to 12, the Samiti collects a **Vidyalaya Vikas Nidhi** of ₹600 a month, from which SC/ST, Divyang and girl students and families below the poverty line are exempt. Check the current amount with the JNV.

## How to apply

1. Download the prospectus from the official NVS admission portal and read the eligibility rules carefully.
2. Keep ready the documents the prospectus asks for, such as the certificate from the child's school head confirming Class 5 study and residence.
3. Register on the portal before the last date given in the prospectus, and save the confirmation.
4. Download the admit card when the portal releases it, and note the test centre.

Only use the **official portal** to apply. Registration is free, so be wary of anyone asking for money to "register" your child.

## Preparing for the test

What follows is SchoolDhundo's guidance, not part of the prospectus:

- **Mental Ability** questions are non-verbal: pattern completion, figure series, mirror and water images, embedded figures. A few questions every day helps more than long sessions once a week.
- **Arithmetic** follows the Class 3 to 5 syllabus. Fractions, measurement, area and perimeter, and reading simple graphs are worth extra practice.
- **Language** is reading comprehension. Reading one short passage a day in the exam language and answering questions on it builds speed.
- Solve the **official 2026 question paper** and the sample questions in the prospectus under real timing (60, 30 and 30 minutes).
- Do not leave any section weak: the qualifying marks apply to each section separately.

## Key points to remember

- One attempt only, in the child's own district, while studying Class 5.
- The date-of-birth window moves forward every year: check it in your year's prospectus.
- Registration is free and only through the official portal.
- Qualifying marks apply to every section, not just the total.
