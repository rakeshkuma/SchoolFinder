Sainik Schools are residential, English-medium schools run by the **Sainik Schools Society** under the Ministry of Defence. They prepare students, among other goals, for officer training academies such as the National Defence Academy. Admission to **Class 6** and **Class 9** is through the **All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE)**, conducted by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**.

This article uses the **AISSEE-2026 information bulletin**. The bulletin for the next cycle may change dates, age limits or rules, so always read the latest one on the NTA website.

> The figures below are from the AISSEE-2026 bulletin, checked on 11 November 2026. When NTA publishes the next bulletin, the age windows will move forward by one year.

## Who can apply

For the 2026 cycle, the bulletin set these age limits (as on 31 March 2026):

| Class | Age | Born between |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class 6 | 10 to 12 years | 1 April 2014 and 31 March 2016 |
| Class 9 | 13 to 15 years | 1 April 2011 and 31 March 2013, and passed Class 8 |

**Girls are eligible to apply.** Seats are split between **Home State** and **Outside State** candidates, based on the child's domicile and where the chosen school is. The bulletin explains this in detail.

## The exam pattern

### Class 6: 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 50 | 150 |
| Language | 25 | 50 |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| General Knowledge | 25 | 50 |

Mathematics carries 3 marks a question; the other subjects 2 marks. The Class 6 paper is offered in English, Hindi and 11 regional languages.

### Class 9: 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 50 | 200 |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| English | 25 | 50 |
| General Science | 25 | 50 |
| Social Science | 25 | 50 |

Mathematics carries 4 marks a question; the other subjects 2 marks. The Class 9 paper is in English only.

## Qualifying marks and selection

The AISSEE-2026 bulletin required at least **25% in each subject and 40% in aggregate**, with category-specific rules described in the bulletin. Merit lists are made **school by school**, by gender and by Home State and Outside State category, followed by e-counselling. A **medical examination** and document check at the allotted school complete admission.

Approved **New Sainik Schools** fill at least 40% of their seats from AISSEE merit.

## Fees

The AISSEE-2026 application fee was **₹850** for General, Defence and OBC (NCL) candidates and **₹700** for SC/ST candidates. School fees are separate and differ from school to school.

## A preparation plan

This is SchoolDhundo's guidance, built on the bulletin's pattern:

1. **Start with the bulletin.** Note the age window, your home state and the schools you can choose. The merit list is per school, so your choice of schools matters.
2. **Give Mathematics the most time.** It is half the marks in Class 6 (150 of 300) and in Class 9 (200 of 400). Work through every topic in the bulletin's syllabus with timed practice.
3. **Practise Intelligence a little every day.** Analogies, series, classification and coding questions improve quickly with regular practice.
4. **Strengthen language and grammar.** For Class 6, revise grammar and comprehension in your chosen medium; for Class 9, English.
5. **Keep a General Knowledge notebook** (Class 6): India, the armed forces, science in daily life, sports and awards.
6. **Solve past papers under exam timing.** NTA publishes final answer keys; practise in 150 or 180 minutes.
7. **Prepare for the medical.** A healthy routine and good eyesight checks before the exam avoid surprises later.

## Key points to remember

- Apply only through the NTA website; read the current bulletin first.
- Age windows change every year.
- Mathematics carries the most marks, but every subject has a minimum.
- Sainik Schools charge fees; ask each school for its fee structure and scholarships.
