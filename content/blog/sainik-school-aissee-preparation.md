Sainik Schools are residential, English-medium schools run by the **Sainik Schools Society** under the Ministry of Defence. They prepare students, among other goals, for officer training academies such as the National Defence Academy. Admission to **Class 6** and **Class 9** is through the **All India Sainik Schools Entrance Examination (AISSEE)**, conducted by the **National Testing Agency (NTA)**.

This article follows the **AISSEE-2027 information bulletin**, which NTA published on 3 November 2026 for admission in the 2027-28 session.

> Dates and rules below are from the AISSEE-2027 bulletin and NTA's public notice of 3 November 2026, checked on 25 November 2026. Always confirm them on the NTA website before applying.

## Key dates for AISSEE-2027

| Step | Date |
| --- | --- |
| Apply online and pay the fee | 3 November to 2 December 2026 (11:50 p.m.) |
| Late applications, with a ₹500 late fee | 3 to 6 December 2026 |
| Corrections to the form | 7 to 9 December 2026 |
| Exam city announced | 11 January 2027 |
| Admit cards on the NTA website | 25 January 2027 |
| Exam | 31 January 2027: Class 6 from 2:00 to 4:30 p.m., Class 9 from 2:00 to 5:00 p.m. |
| Result | To be announced on the NTA website |

Apply only on the NTA AISSEE website, exams.nta.nic.in/sainik-school-society.

## Who can apply

The bulletin sets these age limits for 2027-28 (as on 31 March 2027, both dates included):

| Class | Age | Born between |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Class 6 | 10 to 12 years | 1 April 2015 and 31 March 2017 |
| Class 9 | 13 to 15 years | 1 April 2012 and 31 March 2014, and passed Class 8 from a recognised school at the time of admission |

**Admission for girls is open in Class 6**, and in **Class 9 where vacancies exist**, with the same age limits as boys; the bulletin lists girls' seats school by school. Seats are split between **Home State** and **Outside State** candidates, based on the child's domicile and where the chosen school is. The bulletin explains this in detail.

## The exam pattern

The exam is on paper: multiple-choice questions answered on an OMR sheet. There is **no negative marking**.

### Class 6: 125 questions, 300 marks, 150 minutes

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 50 | 150 |
| Language | 25 | 50 |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| General Knowledge | 25 | 50 |

Mathematics carries 3 marks a question; the other subjects 2 marks. The Class 6 paper is offered in 13 languages: English, Hindi and 11 regional languages.

### Class 9: 150 questions, 400 marks, 180 minutes

| Subject | Questions | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | 50 | 200 |
| Intelligence | 25 | 50 |
| English | 25 | 50 |
| General Science | 25 | 50 |
| Social Science | 25 | 50 |

Mathematics carries 4 marks a question; the other subjects 2 marks. The Class 9 paper is in English only.

## Qualifying marks and selection

The AISSEE-2027 bulletin requires at least **25% in each subject and 40% in aggregate**. In existing Sainik Schools no minimum is prescribed for **SC/ST candidates**, who are admitted on merit within their category; in approved **New Sainik Schools** the 25% and 40% rule applies to everyone, SC/ST included.

Merit lists are made **school by school**, by gender and by Home State and Outside State category, followed by e-counselling. A **medical examination** and document check at the allotted school complete admission. Approved New Sainik Schools fill at least 40% of their seats from AISSEE merit.

## Answer keys

The bulletin says NTA will display the attempted question papers and **provisional answer keys** on its AISSEE website, on a date it will announce. NTA's final answer keys for earlier years are linked from our [AISSEE guide](/exams/aissee/guide).

## Fees

The AISSEE-2027 application fee is **₹850** for General candidates, wards of Defence personnel and ex-servicemen, and OBC (NCL) candidates, and **₹700** for SC/ST candidates. It is not refundable, and late applications pay **₹500** more. School fees are separate and differ from school to school.

## A preparation plan

This is SchoolDhundo's guidance, built on the bulletin's pattern:

1. **Start with the bulletin.** Note the age window, your home state and the schools you can choose. The merit list is per school, so your choice of schools matters.
2. **Give Mathematics the most time.** It is half the marks in Class 6 (150 of 300) and in Class 9 (200 of 400). Work through every topic in the bulletin's syllabus with timed practice.
3. **Practise Intelligence a little every day.** Analogies, series, classification and coding questions improve quickly with regular practice.
4. **Strengthen language and grammar.** For Class 6, revise grammar and comprehension in your chosen medium; for Class 9, English.
5. **Keep a General Knowledge notebook** (Class 6): India, the armed forces, science in daily life, sports and awards.
6. **Solve past papers under exam timing.** NTA publishes final answer keys; practise in 150 or 180 minutes. With no negative marking, attempt every question.
7. **Prepare for the medical.** A healthy routine and good eyesight checks before the exam avoid surprises later.

Practise with free Class 6 mock tests on our [AISSEE preparation page](/exams/aissee), see the syllabus and official answer keys in the [AISSEE guide](/exams/aissee/guide), and find every Sainik School in the [Sainik School directory](/network/sainik-school).

## Key points to remember

- Apply only through the NTA website, by 2 December 2026 (or by 6 December with the late fee).
- Age windows move forward by one year every cycle.
- Mathematics carries the most marks, but every subject has a minimum.
- Sainik Schools charge fees; ask each school for its fee structure and scholarships.
